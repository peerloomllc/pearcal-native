## What's Changed

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

  - Opening the app after a while away is fast again. If you'd left PearCal
    closed for more than a few minutes, it could sit for two to four minutes
    before showing events other people had added. It now catches up within
    seconds. This was most noticeable on iPhone.

  - The desktop app updates itself when your computer wakes up. Previously it
    could stay out of date after your machine had been asleep, until you quit
    and reopened it.
