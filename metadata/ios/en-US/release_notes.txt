## What's Changed

### ✨ Improvements

  - Mexican holidays: Turn on Mexico in Settings > Holidays and your calendar fills in the seven national rest days, plus election day and the presidential
  handover in the years they fall. The three holidays that move to a Monday move on their own.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

  - The home-screen widget shows today again: Overnight it kept yesterday's events under today's date, and opening the app once did not always fix it. It
  now turns the page at midnight on its own, and keeps a week of days ready so it is never showing the wrong one.
  - Blind peers on Start9 start up again: After the StartOS 0.4 update, a blind peer would fail to start and keep restarting because the update moved its
  data folder. It now repairs itself and carries on.
  - Installing a blind peer on Start9 0.4 works: Install and update were failing with a not-found error. The right package is now published for 0.4 boxes.
