## What's Changed

### ✨ New Features

- Automatic calendar export: PearCal can now keep an up-to-date copy of the calendars you choose in a folder. Other apps, such as a backup tool, Nextcloud, Syncthing or a personal assistant, can then read your schedule. Turn it on in Settings. On a phone, the copy updates while PearCal is open and catches up the next time you open it.
