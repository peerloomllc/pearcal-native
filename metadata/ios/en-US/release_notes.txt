What's new in PearCal

  New Features
  - Back up your calendars to PeerLoom Seeder, an always-on helper that keeps your shared calendars available while your phones are off. One seeder now works for all your PeerLoom apps.
  - Choose which calendars go to your seeder, using a switch for each one. Everyone in the group can see who added the seeder.
  - If you already use a PearCal seeder, a short note tells you about PeerLoom Seeder. You can switch to it, keep your current seeder or use both.

  Improvements
  - All your seeders now appear together in one Seeders section in Settings, with a single "Scan seeder QR" button, a "Paste a link instead" option and a link to get PeerLoom Seeder.
  - Adding or removing a seeder now shows clear progress messages, so you know when it is done.
  - When you stop using a seeder, your data stays on it for 7 days before it is deleted, in case you change your mind.

  Bug Fixes
  - PeerLoom Seeder can now be reached when your phone and the seeder are on the same home network.
  - Stopping a seeder now removes every calendar from it. Before, one could be missed.
  - The relay setting no longer sometimes goes missing when the app first opens.
