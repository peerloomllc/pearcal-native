## What's Changed

New
   - Desktop now warns you when a shared calendar is in trouble: when it
    can't sync on this device, when nothing has arrived from the others
    for a while, or when you're waiting to be approved. Calendars
    needing attention are marked in the sidebar. Previously the desktop
    said nothing.

Fixed

    - Desktop: you could change other people's events even when only their
      creator was allowed to. Your change was saved and sent out, leaving a
      version only your computer believed in. Now it opens read-only.
    - Invite links could be created without everything needed to join an
      encrypted calendar, so whoever accepted one could never sync. Links
      are now built correctly, and a device that can't make a working one
      says so instead.
    - Removed a "Rekey Group" button from group settings that could never
      finish.
