## What's Changed

Fixed

  - Fixed the desktop app freezing shortly after you opened it. It
    would show your calendar and then stop responding, or open a shared
    calendar and never show any of its dates. The desktop version had
    been missing several sync repairs the phone apps already had, so it
    also catches up much better now after being closed for a while.

  - Fixed the app slowly using more and more memory the longer it ran,
    until syncing stopped altogether. This usually looked like a
    calendar that had been working fine suddenly going quiet.

  - iPhone: tapping an older-style invite link while PearCal was closed
    opened the app and then did nothing. Normal invite links, and links
    tapped while the app was already open, were not affected.
