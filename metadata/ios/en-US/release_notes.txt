## What's Changed

New Features
  - Open calendar files straight into PearCal. Tap a calendar file (.ics) in your email, Files or another app, or share it to PearCal, and the import screen opens on its own.
  - Desktop: you can now hand a group over to another member, or claim a group whose owner has been inactive for more than 30 days.
  - Desktop: "Reset app data" is now in Settings. Clear your calendar and groups and stay the same person, or start over as a new user.

Improvements
  - Repeating events now carry over when you import a calendar file. Birthdays and other yearly, monthly, weekly or daily events from Google, Apple or Yandex keep repeating instead of showing up only once.
  - Exporting keeps repeating events as a single repeating entry, so "repeat forever" events keep all their future dates and other calendar apps see one series instead of hundreds of copies.
  - Importing a file you already imported no longer creates duplicates of repeating events.
  - If a calendar file has no events PearCal can read, you now get a short message instead of nothing happening.
