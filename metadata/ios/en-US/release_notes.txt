## New Features

  **Reset app data.** You can now clear PearCal from inside the app, under Profile then Settings. There are two options: clear your calendar and groups but stay signed in as yourself, or start over completely as a brand new user. Previously the only way to do this was to delete the app and reinstall it, which on iPhone meant losing everything with no way back.

  **Turn the relay off on desktop.** Mac, Windows and Linux now have the same privacy switch the phone app already had, so you can stop PearCal routing
  through the shared relay if you would rather it did not.

  **See more in the widget.** The home screen widget now shows more of your upcoming events instead of stopping at five, and fits as many as the widget size allows.

  **Know why a join failed.** If joining a group does not work, the app now tells you what went wrong instead of quietly doing nothing.

  ## Privacy

  **Your recovery phrase is no longer stored in iCloud or Google.** Older versions quietly saved it to Apple's or Google's cloud, with no way to see or
  switch that off. It now stays on your device only, and this update automatically deletes any copy that was already uploaded.

  ## Improvements

  **Clearer blind peer pairing.** Pairing now shows progress on both the app and the seeder dashboard, with a countdown, and gets out of your way once it
  succeeds.

  **Accurate blind peer details.** The blind peer list showed how many groups it was seeding at the moment you paired it, which could be badly out of date. It now shows the real number.

  ## Bug Fixes
  
  **Moving an event to another date made it disappear.** The event vanished from your calendar until you restarted the app, and on a paired second device it could be lost for good. Fixed on phone and desktop.

  **Multi-day events dropped out of the widget.** An event spanning several days showed on its first day and then disappeared, even while it was still
  running.

  **Groups could lose the key that keeps them private,** which left you stuck behind a "can't sync" message. Existing affected groups repair themselves, and the desktop app can now repair them too.

  **Re-sharing an event did not bring it back** for anyone else in the group.

  **Invite links were unreliable.** Sometimes tapping one did not open the join screen at all, and older links could leave the app stuck on a blank screen until you force-closed it.

  ## PearCal Seeder

  **Visible on StartOS 0.4.** Adding peerloomllc.com as a custom marketplace on a StartOS 0.4 server showed only PearCircle Seeder. PearCal Seeder now
  appears there and installs correctly. Servers on 0.3.5 were never affected.
