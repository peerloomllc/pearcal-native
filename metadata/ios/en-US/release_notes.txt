## What's Changed

New Features
  - Get your calendars back with your 12 words. On a new phone or computer, choose "I already use PearCal", then "Enter my 12 words". If you use a PeerLoom Seeder, your calendars come back on their own.
  - Your personal events, private notes and reminders come back with your 12 words too.
  - See your 12-word recovery phrase in Settings. Your phone asks for your fingerprint, face or PIN first (Touch ID on a Mac). New users see it once after setup, and existing users see it once after this update.
  - Birthdays: add birthdays in Settings and they show on your calendar every year, with the age when you know the birth year and a reminder that morning.
  - Desktop: you can now pair a PeerLoom Seeder from Settings, so your computer backs up your calendars too.

Improvements
  - Settings now shows whether your calendar list and each of your calendars are fully backed up to your seeder.
  - After a restore, each calendar tells you whether it is waiting for another member to come online. If nobody else was in it, you can make a copy you own.
  - Your seeder now keeps everyone's changes to a shared calendar, not just yours, so a restore brings back the whole calendar.
  - Shared calendars stay in sync more reliably when a member's old device is gone for good.
  - Members you reach through another member now count as online.

Security
  - On Android, screenshots are blocked while your 12 words are on screen.

Bug Fixes
  - Desktop: fixed a crash when opening the app.
  - Desktop: fixed calendars that could not open.
  - Desktop: events set to repeat forever no longer stop after a year.
  - Typing a whole word on the restore screen no longer spills letters into the next word.
