## What's Changed

New

  - Your calendar now tells you when it has stopped syncing with the
    other members, instead of just going quiet. It waits a couple of
    days before saying anything, so it will not nag you when someone
    is simply away for the weekend.

Fixed

  - Fixed the app failing to open, sometimes showing "Failed to start
    PearCal" and sometimes just a blank screen. This could happen after
    closing and reopening quickly.

  - Fixed a device getting stuck when it had fallen behind, where it
    would sit on the loading screen, never catch up, and drain the
    battery. It now fetches what it is missing and stays usable while
    it does.

  - Warnings about a calendar having a problem could disappear moments
    after showing, so you would never see them. They now stay put.

  - Linux: the app would install but never open on Ubuntu 24.04 and
    similar. Fixed.
  
Improved

  - Catching up after being away is much faster and uses far less
    battery.
  
  - Fewer pointless background writes when nothing has actually
    changed.
