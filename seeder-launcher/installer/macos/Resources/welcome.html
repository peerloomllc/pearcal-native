PearCal Seeder

Installs an always-on blind seeder that keeps your PearCal groups in sync even when no other device is online. It replicates encrypted group data without ever holding the key to read it. After install:

  • The seeder launches automatically at login and keeps running via KeepAlive.
  • The monitoring dashboard opens automatically in your default browser.
  • A PearCal Seeder app is added to your Applications — open it any time to reopen the dashboard.
  • To remove everything later, open Uninstall PearCal Seeder in Applications.

To seed a group, open the dashboard and either show a pairing QR to scan in PearCal (Profile → Advanced → Blind peer), or paste a seed invite link from a member.
